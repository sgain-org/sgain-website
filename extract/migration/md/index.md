**Sustainability Governance of China's Global Infrastructure Investments (SGAIN)** is a £1.7 million research project awarded to **Dr [Yixian Sun](https://www.yixiansun.com)** [](https://www.yixiansun.com)at the University of Bath by the UKRI Future Leaders Fellowship.

Our seven-year study programme integrates innovative mixed methods to assess China's efforts to promote green development through overseas infrastructure investments and also environmental and social impacts of key Chinese projects in different host contexts across the Global South. Our research aims to advance understanding of China's overseas engagement and its influence on global sustainable development. Our work will help policy practitioners develop suitable strategies to align international investments with local sustainability needs.

[Learn more](/research)

**Sustainability Governance of China's Global Infrastructure Investments**
==========================================================================

The project team includes leading researchers across the globe in the fields of international relations, environmental governance and development studies based at Fudan University (China), Universitas Gadjah Mada (Indonesia), University of Dhaka (Bangladesh), Sustainable Development Policy Institute (Pakistan), and National University of Singapore (Singapore).
