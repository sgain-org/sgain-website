**Network**
===========

Based on our project, we are building a global knowledge network to involve both researchers and practitioners of sustainability governance. Our primary goal is to facilitate exchange and promote inclusive knowledge production to support global sustainable development. We are particularly interested in engaging with colleagues working on the Global South and China’s global engagement.

Our network will be maintained through a series of channels including newsletters, webinars and also in-person events such as workshops and conferences. Through these activities, we hope to create more opportunities for collaboration. If you are interested in joining us, please sign up **[here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeH30yh9itlUx9K8shQfTvc4-IPz5En0Ms0ZkVtXoKxIYCqVQ/viewform)**!

### **Visiting Doctoral Fellowship**

Our fellowship programme is to support early career researchers (PhD students) who want to work with our project team to conduct research (up to six months) as visiting researchers at the University of Bath. Each year, we will award a few fellows who will receive travel support for their visit at Bath. If you are interested in joining this programme, please submit an application or make inquries at **sgain@bath.ac.uk**.

The application must include:

*   A short research plan (up to 3 pages) with very concrete ideas for at least one research article to be co-authored with our team
    
*   A cover letter stating explaning your motivation and how the fellowship can support your career developement
    
*   Your latest CV
    

We evaluate applications on a recurring basis, but the approval of the fellowship will follow the admin procedures at the University of Bath and may take some time. We advise you to apply 6-9 months before the planned state date of your visit.
