Yixian Sun

Principal Investigator

Dr Yixian Sun is a Professor of Sustainability Governance at the University of Bath and a UKRI Future Leaders Fellow. As a leading expert of environmental governance and sustainable development, his research examines the changing role of emerging economies – especially China – in global environmental governance. He is also an editor of Global Environmental Politics and a member of the Scientific Steering Committee of the Earth System Governance Project. He has published multiple books and over 40 peer-reviewed articles in high-impact international journals. Find here for more details on his work including all publications for free access.
