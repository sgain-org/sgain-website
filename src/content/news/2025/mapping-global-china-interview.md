Earlier this month, Mapping Global China shared an important interview with SGAIN PI Dr Yixian Sun, and Dr Yifei Li, Assistant Professor of Environmental Studies at NYU Shanghai.

This interview provided key climate insights, with topics including China's pivot from large-scale to smaller sustainable projects, the rise of green soft power, and how China's coercive environmentalism impacts citizen behaviour. The full interview is available online: **[here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=br2w5eeqzD8&t=4s)**
