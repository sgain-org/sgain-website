  <h1>Bath Conference on China &amp; Global Sustainability Transition</h1>
  <h6><strong>24th-25th June 2025</strong></h6>
  <p>
    The Bath 2025 conference gathered leading experts on China and
    sustainability from both the Global North and South to discuss opportunities
    and challenges to engage China for accelerating global sustainability
    transition. The conference was organized in partnership with <a
      href="https://www.bath.ac.uk/research-institutes/institute-of-sustainability-and-climate-change/"
      title="https://www.bath.ac.uk/research-institutes/institute-of-sustainability-and-climate-change/"
      >Bath Institute of Sustainability and Climate Change</a
    > and <a href="https://dialogue.earth/en/about/">Dialogue Earth</a>.
  </p>
  <p>
    Understanding China’s fast evolving role in global environmental governance
    requires inputs from experts of all backgrounds and experiences. As such,
    conference participants came from the academic, policy-making, and civil
    society communities, and represented a variety of disciplinary backgrounds.
  </p>
  <p>
    We aim to build a strong global academic-policy network for overcoming
    barriers to understanding China and to chart constructive paths to
    cooperation between China and the rest of the world for sustainable
    development. This 1.5-day conference consisted of keynote speakers, several
    thematic roundtables on China’s changing influence on global sustainability
    governance, and interactive group discussions to gather opinions from all
    participants. The conference was held under the Chatham House Rule.
  </p>
