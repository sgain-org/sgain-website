<p>We are excited to share that in September, Project PI Dr Yixian Sun visited Islamabad to advance dialogue on greening the China-Pakistan Economic Corridor.  </p>
  <p>On September 18th, he attended and spoke at the High-level Conference on Green Industrialization under CPEC 2.0 on China&#39;s enabling role in supporting Pakistan&#39;s green transition. We are grateful to the Sustainable Development Policy Institute for hosting this.</p>
