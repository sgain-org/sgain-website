<p>Project PI Dr Yixian Sun led the University of Bath delegation at COP30. Over the two week event, SGAIN co-organized or joined several events and meetings with our network in Belem.</p>
  <p>Dr Sun spoke about China-Brazil cooperation on green trade and sustainable infrastructure, as well as China’s role in supporting clean energy transition in the Global South. Dr Sun’s COP30 activities included:  </p>
  <p>- 15 November: Can Brazil deliver traceable commodities?<br>- 17 November 9:30-10:45: Sino-Brazilian experiences in forests and infrastructure<br>- 19 November 16:15-17:15: Sunrise on Distributed Renewables and Sunset on Coal Plants: Learnings for Global South</p>
  <p>As part of this important conference, we also launched our <strong><a href="/publications/from-solar-boom-to-green-industrialization-policy-pathways-to-localizing-solar-value-chains-in-pakistan/">first policy report</a></strong> with the <strong><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/company/sustainable-development-policy-institute/">Sustainable Development Policy Institute</a></strong> on solar localization in Pakistan during COP30. The report was featured during the side-events at the Regional Climate Foundations Pavilion.</p>
  <p>During COP30, we also published a new article, <strong><a href="/publications/global-climate-governance-remains-resilient-under-trump-20/">Global climate governance remains resilient under Trump 2.0</a>,</strong> co-authored by Dr Sun and Research Associate Yitong Ye, which emphasises that even in moments when the US steps back, collective climate continues to move forward - driven by a wider set of actors, institutions, and emerging economies.</p>
