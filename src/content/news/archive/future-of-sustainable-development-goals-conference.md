<p>The international research conference GLOBALGOALS2024—The Future of the SDGs, held 29-30 August 2024 in Utrecht, the Netherlands, offered a global platform to share and discuss ideas for the future of Sustainable Development Goals and global sustainablity governance, ahead of the UN Summit of the Future in September.</p>
  <p><strong>Dr Yixian Sun</strong>, gave a keynote talk on Science-Policy Interaction for Sustainable Development. Team member <strong>Faisal Shariff</strong> attended the summer school organised by the conference and presented his first PhD paper on energy justice and the attainment of SDG 7 in Pakistan.</p>
