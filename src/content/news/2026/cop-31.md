At COP31, the University of Bath was represented by Professor Yixian Sun, Director of the Bath Centre for Earth System Governance (BCESG) and a core member of the Bath Institute of Sustainability and Climate Change (ISCC), and Yuen Gu, a PhD researcher on the SGAIN Project and associate member of the BCESG.
Professor Sun took part in a series of dialogues, engaging with policymakers and collaborators. Yuen observed the negotiations and carried out research on the ground, interviewing delegates as part of her PhD on China's South-South climate cooperation.
Their events included:
•	China-Europe Dialogue on Global Climate Governance (13 November), held at the Global Alliance of Universities on Climate Pavilion.
•	Sunrise on Solar, Sunset on Fossil Fuels: Powering Growth through a Just Energy Transition (14 November), held at the Regional Climate Pavilion.
•	Partnerships for Energy Transition: A North-South Dialogue (18 November), a UNFCCC side event organised by the University of Bath with the Sustainable Development Policy Institute (SDPI) and the Kenya Climate Change Working Group Association.
