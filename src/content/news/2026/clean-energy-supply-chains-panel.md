On September 23rd, project PI Yixian Sun took part in the ‘Clean Energy Supply Chains in the Era of Industrial Policy’ policy at Climate Week NYC 2026.

The panel examined one of the defining challenges of the energy transition: how can countries strengthen energy security and domestic industries without slowing the deployment of affordable clean technologies? Discussion connected research across solar PV, offshore wind, and electric vehicles, exploring some of the most important questions facing energy transition. 

Yixian presented SGAIN’s CGEL database and our wider research in conversations around the development of China as a global environmental leader in the last 25 years, and its new efforts in promoting sustainable transition.
