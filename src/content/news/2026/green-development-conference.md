In October 2026, project member Jiuyang Li attended the Green Development Cooperation and Asian Partners Conference, where he presented his paper “Greening the Corridor: Norm Co-Production in China-Pakistan Green Development Cooperation”.

Jiuyang’s paper, co-authored with Yixian Sun, examines China’s interaction with its partners when it promotes an alternative global norm, and if they shape what that norm contains. Looking at China-Pakistan green development cooperation, they find that the norm is co-produced: Pakistan voices demands from outside the environmental domain in the language of green development, and the two sides negotiate which of them enter the jointly signed text. 
This extends Global South agency in norm diffusion from adapting foreign norms to producing normative content, and shows that China's global environmental norms carry its partners' demands alongside its own interests.
Many thanks to DIIS - Dansk Institut for Internationale Studier in Copenhagen for hosting!
