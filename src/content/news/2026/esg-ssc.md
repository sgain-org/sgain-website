We are delighted to share that project PI Yixian Sun has been appointed as a new co-chair of the Earth System Governance Project's Scientific Steering Committee (SSC), alongside Steffen Bauer. 
Together, they will help guide the network's research priorities and strengthen its global community of scholars working on the governance of sustainability transformations.

We are also pleased to announce that our project members Yuen Gu and Jiuyang Li have joined the Earth System Governance Project as research fellows, further deepening our ties to this vibrant research community!
