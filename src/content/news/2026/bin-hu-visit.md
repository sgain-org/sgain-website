In October 2026, Professor Yixian Sun hosted a visit from Professor Bin Hu, Associate Professor and Director of Global Climate Governance Research at the Institute of Climate Change and Sustainable Development (ICCSD), Tsinghua University.

Professor Hu delivered a seminar, hosted by the Bath Centre for Earth System Governance, in collaboration with the ISCC, which explored China's campaign-style approach to tackling air pollution. It also examined how the personal relationships between city-level leaders and their provincial superiors affected the implementation of the environmental inspections and their outcomes.
