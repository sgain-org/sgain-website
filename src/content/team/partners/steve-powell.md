With more than 25 years of experience in global research and evaluation, Steve has led projects from post-tsunami psychosocial programming to East African community resilience. He holds a PhD in psychology on post-war trauma in Bosnia-Herzegovina. As co-founder of Causal Map Ltd., Steve is dedicated to enhancing the collection and synthesis of people's ideas about what influences what.
