Dr Randy Wirasta Nandyatama is an Assistant Professor and head of the Undergraduate Programme in the Department of International Relations at Universitas Gadjah Mada, Indonesia. He received his PhD in 2019 from the University of Melbourne. His research interests span global governance, human rights, ASEAN, and Sino-Southeast Asia relations.
