Dr Maharani Hapsari is an Assistant Professor in the Department of International Relations, Faculty of Social and Political Science, Universitas Gadjah Mada, Indonesia. She has a Master's degree and a PhD from the Graduate School of International Development, Nagoya University, Japan. Her research interests cover knowledge co-production in sustainability transition and the political economy of development.
