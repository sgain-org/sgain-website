Dr Nur Rachmat Yuliantoro is a full professor in the Department of International Relations at Universitas Gadjah Mada. He has studied Chinese foreign policy as one of his areas of research since 2004. His current focus is the development of the Belt and Road Initiative in Indonesia. He can be reached at [nur.rachmat@ugm.ac.id](mailto:nur.rachmat@ugm.ac.id).
