Dr Khalid Waleed holds a PhD in energy economics. With more than 12 years of experience, his expertise includes energy transition at macro and micro levels. He advocates for tailored approaches that cater to specific energy needs and consumption patterns, empowering individuals and communities to participate actively in the acceleration of renewable energy and broader transition efforts.
