Fiona is a communications and research professional with expertise in applying academic research in international development. She has held key global roles, including at Sri Lanka's Centre for Poverty Analysis. Co-author of the QuIP and co-founder of Bath SDR Ltd., Fiona works with Steve to apply her experience from causal mapping QuIP data to the design of Causal Map.
