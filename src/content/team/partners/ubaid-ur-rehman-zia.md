Ubaid ur Rehman Zia is a mechanical engineer specialising in energy systems, currently leading the clean energy transition and CPEC Green Development Programme at SDPI. His areas of expertise include energy forecasting, short- and long-term planning through LEAP and MARKAL/TIMES, investigating and identifying the impact of policy decisions, designing modelling frameworks for both short- and long-term energy planning, and spearheading key projects.
