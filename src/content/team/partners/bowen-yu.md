Dr Bowen Yu is an Associate Professor at the School of International Relations and Public Affairs of Fudan University. He is also an associate editor of _Chinese Political Science Review_ and an affiliated researcher at the Environmental Governance Lab of the University of Toronto. His research focuses on international organisation, global development, global environmental governance, and international relations theory.
