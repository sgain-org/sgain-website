Dr Yixian Sun is a Professor of Sustainability Governance at the University of Bath and a UKRI Future Leaders Fellow. As a leading expert in environmental governance and sustainable development, his research examines the changing role of emerging economies, especially China, in global environmental governance. He is also an editor of _Global Environmental Politics_ and a member of the Scientific Steering Committee of the Earth System Governance Project. He has published multiple books and over 40 peer-reviewed articles in high-impact international journals. Find more details on his work, including all publications available for free access, [here](https://researchportal.bath.ac.uk/en/persons/yixian-sun).
